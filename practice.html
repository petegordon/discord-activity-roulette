Learn to spin

You'll twist your phone to spin the wheel, then flick it to throw the ball, holding it screen-down. While you twist you can't really watch the screen, so this teaches you to spin by ear. Turn your volume up (on iPhone, turn Silent Mode off: it mutes web pages).

On iPhone, allow motion access when asked.
